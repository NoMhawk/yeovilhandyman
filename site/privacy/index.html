Privacy notice

This notice explains what Yeovil Handyman does with the details you give us through this website.

Who we are

Yeovil Handyman, Yeovil, Somerset. You can contact us about your data at web@yeovilhandyman.com.

What we collect and why

When you send an enquiry we receive your name, email address, and anything else you choose to add (phone number, area, details of the job). We use this only to reply to you, give you a quote and arrange any work you ask for. We don't sell it or pass it to anyone else for marketing.

How the contact form works

The form is run by Web3Forms, which passes your message to us by email. Your message is then kept in our email account.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries only for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and any work that follows, and for any records we must keep by law.

Website analytics and cookies

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't identify you. The site is hosted by Cloudflare. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to see the details we hold about you, ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete them, by emailing web@yeovilhandyman.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
